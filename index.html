What you can do
DataDashboardShare

Bring in your data

CSV, JSON, Parquet, spreadsheets, SQL databases and REST APIs. Clean and reshape it with a guided, visual flow that shows you the result as you go and never touches your original file.

Build on an endless canvas

40+ chart types, KPI tiles, slicers and multi-page sheets on a canvas you can zoom and drag. Publish once and it fits whatever screen it opens on.

Chat with AI

Ask about your data in plain English and get trends, forecasts and correlations without writing a query. It runs on your machine by default and asks before it reads anything.

On your device*
Apple Intelligence MLX Ollama
Models you can run†
Cloud, your own key‡

See it on a map

Turn addresses, regions and coordinates into choropleths, flight paths and 3D globes, so location stops being a column and starts being an answer.

Keep it private

Projects are encrypted on disk, sensitive ones lock behind Touch ID or Windows Hello and every export carries a tamper-evident record you can verify.

Make it yours

Restyle every chart and the whole app from a palette marketplace, in light, dark and OLED or pair MyBI with a client, university or company logo.

Talk to it out loud

Ask your question out loud and hear the answer back. Both halves run on your own machine and you pick the engine for each.
